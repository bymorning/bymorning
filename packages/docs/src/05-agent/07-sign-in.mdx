Sign in to reach your Workspace. A new account gets a `Default` Workspace and `Default` Project.

## Sign in

1. Open your ByMorning URL.
2. Select **Sign in** and complete the identity provider flow.
3. ByMorning opens your first available Workspace.

A sign-in link with a saved `redirect` returns you to that page. The first account is the owner of the new `Default` Workspace.

[Start a Session](/agent/start-a-session) from the `Default` Project.

## Log out

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, then select **Log Out**.

To change Workspace without logging out, [switch Workspaces](/agent/switch-workspaces).
